# 20 November 2026 — The World in One Day

Entry for make.fun's `news-image-20261120`: one image summing up the world's
news of Friday 20 November 2026. The image is original work, drawn as
HTML/CSS/SVG and rendered to PNG with headless Chromium; no photographs or
copied artwork are used.

## What is in the image

- **41 verified stories** from 20 November 2026: 11 numbered lead stories,
  each with its own hand-drawn SVG illustration, plus a 30-item "also on"
  list covering Asia, Europe, the Americas, Africa, the Middle East,
  Oceania and global stories — politics, war, markets, science, weather,
  culture and sport.
- **Lead stories:** Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's east coast with
  300,000 evacuated; Anthropic closing 17% up on its $83 IPO debut;
  Wall Street's sell-off deepening (Dow −1,101); Glenn Phillips' 118
  lifting New Zealand to 317–6 at lunch in Wellington; the COP31 draft
  dropping the fossil-fuel phase-out; Haiti forces retaking the
  gang-held Martissant corridor; the UK–EU summit opening in Brussels;
  Musk saying "I'm not MAGA"; Trump pardoning Pete Rose; Iraq's PM
  moving against pro-Iran militias; six killed in a Georgia gas blast.
- **A dotted world map** with numbered pins for the lead stories, four stat
  boxes (S&P 500, Dow, Anthropic, Bitcoin), a "not today" date-check strip
  (things reported on the 19th, not the 20th), and a sources line.

Every story was first reported on 20 November 2026 (sources: AP, Reuters,
AFP, BBC, Nikkei Asia, CNA, The Hindu, The Standard, NDTV, PACNEWS).
Anthropic's market valuation is deliberately not stated — sources
disagreed on it — only the agreed figures: $97.17 close, $111.50 open,
$81.3bn raised.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the image (1024x1024)
- `poster.html` — the HTML/CSS/SVG source of the front page
- `main.py` — Python 3.13, standard library only; copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` after checking the PNG signature,
  dimensions and size. Runs with no network; both runs write identical
  bytes.
